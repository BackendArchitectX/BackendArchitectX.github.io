02 / Design record

Traceability matters more than pretending the demo is production-complete.

The repository is explicit about what is implemented today and what belongs on the roadmap.

01

Reason-coded decisions

Risk outputs include a score, risk band, decision and reason codes so a reviewer can understand why the rule engine reached a result.

Property: explainability.
02

Audit trail

Decision events capture trace references, actions and timestamps to make operational decisions reviewable.

Property: traceability.
03

Simple persistence on purpose

The current public version uses an in-memory store for easy local execution; PostgreSQL/MySQL persistence is an explicit extension rather than an invented capability.

Property: honest scope boundary.
