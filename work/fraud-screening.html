02 / Design record

The useful part is the constraint behind each mechanism.

Patterns only matter when they protect a concrete invariant.

01

Duplicate-safe processing

Idempotency checks prevent retries from turning one logical transaction into repeated fraud evaluation side effects.

Invariant: same request identity should not create duplicate work.
02

Burst admission control

Token-bucket rate limiting protects the service and dependencies during short traffic spikes instead of letting queues grow without bound.

Failure mode: overload propagation.
03

Dependency isolation

Circuit breakers, timeouts and fallbacks stop a slow external risk provider from consuming the entire latency budget.

Failure mode: external latency dominates request path.
04

Operational feedback loop

CloudWatch, structured logging, alerting, runbooks, incident triage and RCA make production behavior part of the engineering loop.

Result: 99.8% uptime and MTTR 35 → 12 min.
This page intentionally avoids proprietary schemas, rule logic and provider-specific details. The architecture is generalized from resume-supported responsibilities.
